The loop

Tap → your garden grows → look back.

TileArc is built around one tiny daily ritual. Everything else — streaks, stats, companions — grows out of your own taps. Nothing is reconstructed, nothing is imported from somewhere else.

01 — TAP

One tap fills the day

Open Today's Page, or tap right on the home-screen widget. Each habit is a tile; filling it takes a second. Miss a day and the tile simply stays empty — no guilt trips, no broken chains held against you.

02 — GROW

Streaks with a soft voice

Days in a row become a glowing lantern — or a rising combo, if you picked the quest life. Your companion notices: Mochi celebrates petals, Kaito hypes panels. Gentle reminders nudge only the habits you choose.

03 — LOOK BACK

A year you can hold

Every tap lands on a year grid — 365 tiles of evidence. Add a diary line about the day, zoom into the 12-week mini-grid, and export it all as JSON whenever you like. Your history is yours, portable forever.

The year grid

Every day, a tile. Every year, a story.

The grid is the heart of TileArc. One cell per day, coloured deeper the more complete the day — with per-habit detail underneath, streak lists, and space for a line of diary.

🗓️

Full-year view + 12-week mini-grid

See the whole arc at a glance, then zoom into the last twelve weeks for texture. Deeper colour means a fuller day — computed from your entries, never guessed.

🔥

Streaks, current and best

“Current glow: 6 days. Longest glow: 21 days.” Full streak lists per habit, plus trend views that show whether this month is beating last month.

📖

Diary Entries & Battle Log

A line about today, attached to the date — a diary page in Sakura, a battle report in Shonen. Notes travel with exports, so your words are never locked in.

📦

Export & import that respects you

One JSON file holds habits, entries, notes and settings. Export anytime, import on a new device — the same open format works across platforms.

Gentle by default

Nudges you asked for. Nothing you didn't.

Reminders are per-habit, optional, and local to your iPhone. Pick weekdays and a time — “a gentle nudge, if you'd like one” — and days you already logged stay silent.

A — CHOOSE

Weekdays + a time

Set reminders while planting a habit or later in Settings. Each habit keeps its own schedule; turn any of them off without touching the rest.

B — LOCAL ONLY

Your iPhone reminds you

Notifications are scheduled on-device. There is no server watching your streaks, no push campaign, no “we miss you” email — because TileArc has no idea who you are.

C — SMART QUIET

Done days stay silent

If you already filled a habit, its reminder for that day is skipped. The nudge only speaks when there is still a petal — or panel — left to fill.

Privacy

Yours. On your iPhone.

TileArc has no account, no backend, and no analytics. The only network use in the app is the App Store itself, at purchase time. Read the full policy — written for humans and for App Review.

🔒

No account, no server

Habits, entries, notes and settings live in a JSON file on your device. There is nowhere for them to leak to, because there is no TileArc server.

📡

No analytics, no tracking

TileArc phones home to nobody. No event logging, no identifiers for ads, nothing that follows you between apps. StoreKit talks to Apple only when you buy or restore Pro.

🗑️

Delete means delete

Remove a habit, clear an entry, or delete the app — your data goes with it. Exports are files you create, kept wherever you put them.
