Privacy

Your journeys are yours.

Jaunt Log is designed around your personal data, not around collecting it. Your history is primarily managed on your device — no social profile, no account required, no advertising-focused tracking experience.

◉Local-first
Jaunts, notes and progress are stored locally using SwiftData and an offline spatial index. No Jaunt Log backend is required to use the app.
⬡Photos stay with you
When you allow photo access, Jaunt Log reads supported date and location metadata on-device. Originals remain in your Photos library and are not sent to a Jaunt Log server.
⬔No account required
Start exploring without creating another online profile.

We don’t claim “nothing ever leaves your device.” For example, map tiles and optional diagnostics may involve network requests as described in the app’s Privacy screen. See the in-app Privacy section for the accurate, current wording.
