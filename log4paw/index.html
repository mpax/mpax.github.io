Why Log4Paw

Stress-aware, reliable, honest.

During a seizure you need one big, obvious button — not a form. Afterwards you need a short, guided record. Log4Paw is built for one-handed, stressful moments first, and for calm review with your vet second.

Time in one tap

A large coral timer on Today. Persisted before any animation, accurate through background, lock, termination and relaunch — with a Lock Screen Live Activity where supported.

— No pause • no silent loss

Record without stress

A 4-step form with per-step drafts: timing, what you observed, before & after, care & media. Observation chips — never a diagnosis question. Save a basic record or add details.

— Observation, not diagnosis

Share when you choose

Trends, a date-range report builder and one-tap PDF + CSV sharing for your vet. Full JSON backup with media option. Sharing is the moment data leaves your phone — and it only happens when you tap Share.

Privacy

Your records stay with you.

Log4Paw has no account and no cloud service and does not automatically send information to anyone. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no network calls. Everything works in Airplane Mode.

◉On this iPhone
SwiftData records + protected media storage. If you delete the app without exporting a backup, records and media may be lost.
⬡You choose when data leaves
Only via Export or Share you tap. Sharing sends a copy outside this iPhone — only share what you intend your vet or caregiver to receive.
⬔Permissions in context
Notifications only after you enable reminders; camera only if you tap capture. PhotosPicker needs no broad library access. No tracking prompt — there is nothing to track.

The in-app About & privacy screen shows the same wording as this site. See the full Privacy Policy for App Store review and details.
