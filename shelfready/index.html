How it works

From product idea to safety-file confidence in four steps.

1 Add product

Create a product file

Name your product, add the SKU, batch size, and intended use. ShelfReady keeps every detail in one place so nothing gets lost between batches.

One file per product
2 Checklist

Track what you need

Mark off safety substantiation steps as you complete them. ShelfReady highlights gaps so you always know what still needs attention.

Gap spotting, not guessing
3 Attach

Store supporting docs

Attach certificates of analysis, GMP records, supplier statements, and photos. Everything stays local on your device.

Photos, PDFs & more
4 Export

PDF in one tap

When an inspector, retailer, or regulator asks, export a clean PDF safety file and share it however you already share files.

Share-ready in seconds
✓ Repeat

Keep every product shelf-ready

Update files as formulas evolve, attach new records, and re-export whenever you need. Your vault grows with your line — without growing your workload.
