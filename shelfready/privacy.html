Privacy Policy for ShelfReady

Last updated:

ShelfReady is built around a simple idea: your cosmetics safety files are your business. We don't want your data, and we don't collect it.

In short

Everything you enter into ShelfReady — product names, formulas, supplier documents, photos, checklists, and exports — is stored only on your device. There is no account to create, no cloud database, and no analytics or advertising tracking.

1. What ShelfReady stores

ShelfReady stores the information you intentionally add to build product safety files:

  • Product names, SKUs, batch sizes, and intended uses
  • Checklist progress and notes
  • Attached documents, certificates, and photos
  • PDF exports you generate
  • A local record of export dates (used only to show export history inside the app)

All of this is kept in a local database on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's SwiftData framework. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share or back it up.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics, crash data, or diagnostics (other than what Apple may provide to us through App Store Connect, which is aggregated and not personally identifiable)
  • Your product formulations, supplier information, or attachments

3. Subscriptions

ShelfReady offers a free tier and an optional paid subscription. Payments and subscription status are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not process or store your payment information, and we do not have access to your Apple ID.

4. Sharing and exports

When you tap "Export PDF" or use the iOS share sheet, ShelfReady creates a file and hands it to the standard iOS sharing system. From there, you control where it goes — Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop, printing, or another app. We do not see, intercept, or retain a copy of anything you share.

5. Data backup

If you have iCloud Backup or local device backups enabled for your iPhone or iPad, your ShelfReady data may be included in those Apple-managed backups, just like any other app data. This is under your control through your device settings. ShelfReady does not operate its own backup service.

6. Your rights and choices

Because all data is local, you are always in control:

  • You can view, edit, or delete any product or attachment at any time.
  • You can delete all ShelfReady data from within the app's Settings screen.
  • You can uninstall the app, which removes its data from your device.

If you have questions about data you have shared outside the app, please contact the recipient directly.

7. Children's privacy

ShelfReady is intended for use by adults operating cosmetics businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app evolves. If we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Because ShelfReady does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly; please check this page occasionally.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how ShelfReady handles data, please contact us at hello@m-pax.net.

ShelfReady is published by M-Pax. This policy applies to the ShelfReady iOS app (bundle identifier net.m-pax.ShelfReady).
