how it works

three quiet steps.
nothing else.

this is not a feed, a streak, or a social network. it is one continuous space: home is the present, capture is a layer above it, and moments are a layer behind it. no tab bar. no chrome.

01 — notice

this

open the app and you are already there. the wordmark types itself, the time reads quietly underneath, and a single breathing circle waits at the bottom. tap it — or don’t. being here is enough.

“this is your first moment. tap the circle to notice the present.”

02 — keep

here

answer one question: what is here right now? a word, a sentence — or nothing at all. add a photo if the light asks for it. gentle fragments drift in when you’re stuck. then keep it.

“coffee steam, morning light.” · keep this · keep the silence · keep this image

03 — remember

moments

not a feed — a quiet month grid. one dot under a day means something was noticed there. tap a dotted day to read it back below. a silent moment is just a dot: i was here.

“the light came in sideways.” · “coffee, still hot.” · ●

the app

a quiet space,
not another feed.

four screens. four sentences. everything else stays out of the way.

details

calm by design.

every choice removes something: no likes, no counts, no streaks, no notifications begging you back. even the delete button hides behind three faint dots — releasing a moment takes a breath, not a tap.

what it refuses to do

  • —no feed. moments are read day by day, never scrolled endlessly.
  • —no streaks. missing a day changes nothing. the grid simply stays quiet.
  • —no noise. no sounds that do nothing, no badges, no prompts to “engage”.
  • —no accounts. nothing to sign into, nothing to follow, nothing to perform.

what it gently offers

  • ·silent moments. keep nothing at all — a single dot that says i was here.
  • ·drifting fragments. quiet, light, still — small words that arrive only when you’re stuck, and leave the moment you type.
  • ·three colours, two nights. paper, stone and clay — each with a light and a dark face that follows your phone, or stays pinned.
  • ·still when you need it. breathing pauses and typewriter lines settle instantly with Reduce Motion.
