Currency & recency engine

Every number shows its working.

Rules are versioned data bundles, not code. FAA §61.57(a) day, §61.57(b) night (full-stop, 1-hour-after-sunset window), §61.57(c) instrument (6 approaches + holding + intercept/track in 6 calendar months), §61.56 flight review — alongside FCL.060(b), SEP revalidation, LAPL recency and medical alerts.

  • ✓Evidence on tap. Contributing entries with qualifying values; excluded near-misses with reasons (“PICUS unsigned”).
  • ✓Gap check before the checkride. Missing types, unsigned records, expired signatory certificates, review/medical within the goal window — with pre-written chase text via share sheet.
  • ✓Quiet and exact. Tabular numerals, no gamification, no streaks. Reduce Motion respected.

Copy rule: “On your entries, you meet §61.57(a) for ASEL until 3 Dec 2026.” Never “you are legal to fly.” First engine use and every report footer carry the full disclaimer.

Aircraft, aerodromes and instructors feed every form, endorsement and report. Aircraft classification snapshots onto the entry — later edits never rewrite history silently. All instants UTC; display renders local or decimal hours. Durations are whole minutes; there is no floating-point time math.

Reports & proof pack

Reports are the product.

Generated on-device. Every figure carries profile + bundle version + window; every page is numbered with pilot name + licence number. Any element can be generated alone — or as a single proof pack (PDF + optional CSV bundle).

R1 FAA logbook

§61.51(b) fields, profile totals with page + cumulative. Cross-country computed under the certificate’s own definition (>50 NM and friends are rule data, not code).

R2 FCL.050 logbook

Front matter, landscape 12 groups, page-totals rows reconciling exactly in every time column, verbatim certification block on every page, countersignatures in remarks.

R3 UK CAA submission

FCL.050 paginated for A4 + personal-details page + one certification page (date, full name, signature, CAA reference) — signed in-app or by hand after printing.

R4 FAA 8710 worksheet

Form 8710-1 Section III-style grid for the selected certificate/rating, every cell drill-downable in-app. Labelled as a transfer worksheet; nothing is submitted.

R5 Endorsements + signatures

AC 61-65-style versioned templates, pre-filled from linked entries, signature images where the profile accepts electronic signing. Gap check flags what an examiner will look for.

R6 Raw CSV — no lock-in

One row per flight / FSTD / ground entry with every stored fact (UTC ISO-8601, integer minutes, four landing counts, countersignature states) + documented column dictionary. Export works paid or not.

Reconciliation gate (hard gate, not a warning): if any page total fails to reconcile in integer minutes, export blocks and names the failing column/page. FSTD appears in FCL group 11 and training-received totals — never in total time of flight. Generated files are stored, shareable, deletable, never silently regenerated.
Honest labelling — what Log4Pilot is not
  • Not an EFB — no planning, weather, NOTAMs, navigation, ADS-B or weight & balance. Record-keeping and proof aid only.
  • Not “approved by FAA / EASA / CAA” — authorities accept records; they do not approve apps. Copy says “formats designed to match …”.
  • No filing with any authority — the app prepares worksheets and evidence; submission happens elsewhere.
  • Not legal advice — responsibility remains with the pilot, instructor and examiner (see disclaimer below).

Privacy & data safety

Private by design. Yours forever.

No accounts, analytics, tracking or third-party network SDKs. Optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock. Signature and licence images live on-device and in your own iCloud backup only. Crash diagnostics via Apple opt-in mechanisms only.

  • ✓Automatic backup, visible health. Versioned archive (JSON + blobs + manifest) to the app’s iCloud Drive container. Failure raises a local notification — silent backup failure is release-blocking.
  • ✓Restore with a safety net. Pre-restore snapshot, manifest + totals reconciliation before commit. Manual export any time — regardless of entitlement.
  • ✓Pay once, keep everything. Entitlement gates new entries beyond the free allowance only. Viewing, searching, exporting, reporting and restoring are never gated.

How it works

From shutdown to proof pack.

Built offline-first in SwiftUI for iOS 17+ (iPhone-first). Pure-function projection + currency engines over value-type snapshots — deterministic and fixture-tested. PDF via on-device renderer; CSV via hand-rolled, escape-tested writer.

Seed once

Directories + balances

Add aircraft, aerodromes (built-in GA gazetteer + your strips), instructors. Import past flying via generic CSV mapping or asserted opening balances — labelled, lifetime-only, never currency.

Fly + log

30 seconds a flight

Quick-log from Today. Local notifications for currency, medical, pending countersignatures and backup health — requested in context, never for marketing.

Hand over

Proof, not promises

Generate the pack an examiner expects — cover with profiles + rule versions + timestamp + disclaimer, evidence lists, signed pages. If it doesn’t reconcile, it doesn’t export.
