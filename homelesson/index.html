Home-education planner & evidence log • iPhone + iPad

One app for the whole of home education.

HomeLesson helps a home-educating parent plan the term, run the school day, capture evidence of learning, and produce a local-authority-ready record — for every subject, not just one.

Local-first privacy: records stay private unless you export them. No account, no cloud sync, no analytics. The parent is the teacher — confidence is tracked by parent, never a grade, prediction or assessment.

Why HomeLesson

Calm, reliable, honest.

Five promises, stated up front in the app: one app for all home education; proof without paperwork; know what’s covered; a guided GCSE maths path; reliable and private. The app organises and evidences learning — it never claims to assess your child or replace your judgement.

Run the day in one tap

Session timer + timetable

Start timing this session now.

No navigation, no form, no decisions when learning starts. The timer is the source of truth — absolute start and end dates — so the duration survives anything iOS does to the app. Today also shows the week’s schedule and recent sessions, so evidence builds itself as you log.

  • ✓Weekly timetable, per child — terms, a Monday–Sunday grid and slot editor. Subject is prefilled from the timetable when a slot matches now.
  • ✓Local reminders, offline — up to 8 rolling reminders per slot with stable IDs, rebuilt on edit. Permission is asked only after you enable a reminder, never at first launch.
  • ✓Evidence without effort — session notes and photos of handwritten work attach in seconds; the session appears in history, hours and attendance immediately.

Privacy

Your records stay with you.

Records stay private unless you export them. HomeLesson has no account and no cloud service and does not automatically send information to anyone. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no network calls. Everything works in Airplane Mode.

◉On this device
SwiftData records + protected media storage. If you delete the app without exporting a backup, records and photos may be lost — the app tells you this and nudges you to back up.
⬡You choose when data leaves
Only via Export or Share you tap — a PDF you send the council, a JSON backup you keep. Only share what you intend the recipient to receive.
⬔Children’s records, parent-held
No child accounts, no ads, no profiling, no social features. A parent creates and holds the logbook on their own device. See the full policy for the App Store review details.
